# olympiad-academy-app — project orientation

## What this is

- AI-assisted math olympiad tutoring product. First pilot: a narrow, reversible MVP for beginner Grade 5 students preparing for their first Math Olympiad, focused on Uzbekistan. Internal demo target: 12 September 2026. — provenance: operator_locked

## People and needs

- Primary user: beginner Grade 5 students, first-time Math Olympiad participants (narrowed from an earlier, too-broad "grades 5-11" framing). A teacher validates problems/solutions/hints and evaluates the tutor's behavior. — provenance: operator_locked

## Core experience

- One complete flow: diagnostic assessment into guided practice, over a small teacher-validated problem set, with a simple map of prerequisite skills instead of full Graph RAG. — provenance: operator_locked

## Product areas

- Content pipeline, AI tutor/retrieval, diagnostic/leveling, localization (UI in three languages, learning content starting in one). Detail moves into dedicated area topics as each is explored. — provenance: operator_locked

## Principles and boundaries (out of MVP scope)

- No voice, no photo-based solution checking, no mobile apps, no multi-subject support, no payments, no complex gamification, no full Graph RAG. — provenance: operator_locked

## Success signal

- A successful internal demo on 12 September 2026 showing the full diagnostic-to-practice flow for the narrow pilot segment; further scope is evidence-driven afterward, not pre-committed. — provenance: operator_locked

## Business and operating context

- One shared GitHub repository, Linear for tasks, Vibe Engineer as the common workflow; tiered model-by-task-difficulty policy; individual developer access only, nothing shared; no real children's personal data in dev tools. — provenance: operator_locked

## Roadmap direction

- Long-term vision is broader (more grades, subjects, olympiad platforms) but explicitly deferred beyond this MVP. — provenance: operator_locked

## Unresolved tensions (honest unknowns)

- Primary agentic harness not finally settled team-wide (scaffolded with Pi; day-to-day work currently via Claude Code). Validated learning-content language for the first demo, the AI tutor's exact role/boundaries, and the exact multilingual demo scope are all still open. — provenance: unknown
